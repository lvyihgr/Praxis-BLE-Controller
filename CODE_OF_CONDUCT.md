# Code of conduct

Praxis contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces
welcoming, respectful and focused on constructive technical collaboration.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, publication of private
information and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Project
maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through GitHub. Reports
will be reviewed fairly and confidentially to the extent practical.
